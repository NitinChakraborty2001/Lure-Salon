# Lure Salon landing page

## Goal
Create a luxurious, welcoming one-page website that turns Dallas visitors into booked guests while making Lure’s service quality, team expertise, and environmental commitments immediately clear.

## Experience
- Build the selected **Editorial Sage Luxe** direction with warm cream, sage, deep green, and restrained gold; pair elegant editorial type with clean body text.
- Lead with Lure Salon, Dallas/established 2010, a high-quality custom balayage portrait, and direct actions to book online or call **214-919-5873**.
- Keep appointment actions easy to reach throughout the page and add a compact mobile booking bar without covering content.
- Use subtle entrance and image motion with reduced-motion support.

## Page content
- Present the complimentary consultation and new-client gift prominently, with accurate 7-business-day adjustment language and no implication of refunds.
- Highlight Balayage, Japanese Head Spa, Vivids, Hair Extensions, Bridal Services, and Treatments + Textures; include the remaining services in a concise expanded list.
- Feature Davines, Kérastase, Oribe, and Lure Co+Op without inventing unsupported product claims.
- Give strong visual weight to Green Circle certification, 95% waste recycling, 100% renewable energy, women-owned certification, and community support.
- Introduce Yvonne Hager prominently and include the full supplied team roster in a polished, scannable directory without fabricating staff portraits or biographies.
- Add location, email, social links, WiFi details, full cancellation/adjustment/product exchange policies, and a current-year footer.

## Conversion and trust
- Send booking actions to `https://luresalon.zenoti.com/webstoreNew/services/` and call actions to `214-919-5873`.
- Avoid unsupported ratings, review counts, prices, hours, testimonials, and urgency claims.
- Label the offer plainly and use reassuring, premium language rather than aggressive sales copy.

## Technical details
- Replace the placeholder homepage at `/` and define semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Generate and bundle cohesive salon imagery matching the selected direction instead of shipping placeholders or hotlinked images.
- Load Fraunces and the supporting sans-serif through document links, and add unique Lure Salon title, description, Open Graph, Twitter, and canonical metadata on the home route.
- Keep all links keyboard-accessible, ensure strong contrast, responsive text/layout, descriptive image alternatives, and visible focus states.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile widths, confirm the booking/call/contact links, and check the latest build and browser error signals.
